LowLag — third-party components
================================

LowLag itself (the LowLag application, its network core and user interface) is proprietary
software. It ships together with, or installs, the following independent programs and
libraries. Each keeps its own license.

1. Sunshine — game streaming host
   License: GNU General Public License v3.0
   Version: v2026.914.233613 (installed as a separate program in "Program Files\Sunshine")
   Source:  https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine/tree/v2026.914.233613

2. Moonlight (moonlight-qt) — game streaming client
   License: GNU General Public License v3.0
   Version: 6.2.0, unmodified official portable build (folder "moonlight")
   Source:  https://github.com/moonlight-stream/moonlight-qt/tree/v6.2.0
   Moonlight includes Qt (LGPL v3), FFmpeg (LGPL/GPL), SDL2 (zlib), Opus (BSD) and other
   libraries; their notices are distributed by the Moonlight project.

   LowLag starts Sunshine and Moonlight as separate programs and talks to them only through
   their command line and HTTP interfaces. For three years from the date you received this
   copy we will provide, on request and at no charge beyond the cost of distribution, the
   complete corresponding source code of the GPL components exactly as distributed.

3. Wintun — virtual network adapter driver (wintun.dll)
   Copyright (C) WireGuard LLC. Distributed under the "Prebuilt Binaries License".
   https://www.wintun.net

4. Fonts "Onest" and "Unbounded" — SIL Open Font License 1.1
   (license texts in the application's ui/fonts folder)

5. Rust libraries (MIT and/or Apache-2.0): tauri, wintun (Rust bindings), chacha20poly1305,
   chacha20, x25519-dalek, hkdf, sha2, rand, ureq, native-tls, socket2, if-addrs, igd-next,
   serde, serde_json, base64, log, windows-sys and their dependencies.

6. Rendezvous: connection codes are matched through several public servers used in
   parallel: ntfy (https://ntfy.sh) and the public MQTT brokers broker.emqx.io,
   broker.hivemq.com and test.mosquitto.org. Only small end-to-end-encrypted messages are
   exchanged there; all streaming traffic flows directly between the two computers.
